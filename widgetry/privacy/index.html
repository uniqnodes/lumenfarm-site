▸ Widgetry · Privacy Policy · Effective November 22, 2026

Your widgets stay on your iPhone.

Widgetry: Widgets & Icons (“Widgetry”, “we”) is built so that we never see your data. This policy explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Widgetry does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Widgetry makes no network requests of its own.

Calendar and Reminders

If you make a calendar or reminders widget, Widgetry asks for access so the widget can show your next events or today’s reminders. It only reads them; it never creates, changes or deletes anything. A short summary of upcoming items (titles and times) is kept on your iPhone so your widgets can update without opening the app. It is cleared when you delete those widgets in Widgetry, when you turn access off, and when you delete the app.

Photos

Photo widgets use the system photo picker, so Widgetry only sees the photos you pick. Copies of those photos are stored inside Widgetry on your iPhone for your widgets. If you save a theme’s wallpaper, Widgetry asks for permission to add one image to Photos; it never reads your library.

Icons

Theme icons are saved as image files in the Files app (On My iPhone › Widgetry). You add them to your Home Screen yourself with the Shortcuts app; Widgetry never installs profiles.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

Widgetry does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

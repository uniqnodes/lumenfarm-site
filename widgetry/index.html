Common questions

How do I put a widget on my Home Screen?

Save your widget in Widgetry, then touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, choose Widgetry and the size you made. A new widget shows your latest design of that size; to pin a different one, touch and hold the widget › Edit Widget › Design.

Do my widgets work in Dark, Tinted and Clear modes?

Yes. Every look has its own dark version, and in Tinted and Clear iOS draws your widget in one tint, which Widgetry is designed for. The editor’s mode switch shows each mode before you save.

How do theme icons work?

iOS only lets you change an app icon by hand, with the Shortcuts app. Widgetry saves each icon to Files and walks you through it one app at a time. Icon sets are designed for the Default Home Screen; in Tinted and Clear modes iOS shows Shortcuts icons as plain tiles.

What happens if Pro ends?

Everything you made keeps working, and you can still edit it. New Pro looks, themes and photos beyond the free amount need Pro again.

Restore purchases / cancel a subscription

Open Widgetry › Settings (gear) › Restore Purchases. To cancel the monthly or yearly plan, go to iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Widgetry at least 24 hours before it renews. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Widgetry upload anything?

No. Widgetry has no account, no servers and no analytics; your calendar, reminders and photos stay on your iPhone.

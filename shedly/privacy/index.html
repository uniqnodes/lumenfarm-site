▸ Shedly · Privacy Policy · Effective November 19, 2026

Your photos never leave your iPhone.

Shedly: Photo Cleaner (“Shedly”, “we”) is built so that we never see your data. This policy explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Shedly does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Photo library access

Shedly asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos, and so it can delete the items you choose or save a compressed copy of a video. All of this analysis runs entirely on your device using Apple’s frameworks. Photos, videos and the results of the analysis are never uploaded anywhere.

Shedly keeps a small on-device cache of analysis results (numbers that describe each photo) to make later scans faster. It stays on your iPhone, is removed when you delete the app, and can be cleared any time in Settings › Rescan Library.

Deleting photos

Shedly only deletes items you select, and iOS asks you to confirm every deletion. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is on, deletions sync to your other devices, exactly as in the Photos app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

Shedly does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

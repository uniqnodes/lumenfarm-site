Common questions

Where do deleted photos go?

Everything you delete with Shedly goes to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and stays there for 30 days. You can recover it from there. iOS asks you to confirm every deletion.

Why did less space free up than Shedly showed?

Shedly shows the size of items in your photo library. If iCloud Photos with “Optimize iPhone Storage” is on, some originals live only in iCloud, so the space freed on the iPhone itself can be smaller. Space is also only released once items leave Recently Deleted.

Does deleting in Shedly delete from my other devices?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is on. Shedly uses the standard Photos library, so deletions sync like they do in the Photos app.

Does Shedly upload my photos?

No. All analysis happens on your iPhone. Shedly has no account, no servers and no analytics.

Restore purchases / cancel a subscription

Open Shedly › Settings › Restore Purchases. To cancel the yearly or monthly plan, go to iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Shedly at least 24 hours before it renews. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

How do I make a ringtone?

Pick a video, a music file or a recording, move the bracket onto the part you want (up to 30 seconds), then tap Set as Ringtone. In the share sheet choose Use as Ringtone. iOS adds it to Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone under the name you gave it.

Why does Ringsmith ask “Did it work?”

iOS doesn’t tell apps whether the ringtone was added, replaced or skipped as a duplicate, so Ringsmith asks you once instead of guessing.

Can I use songs from Apple Music or Spotify?

No. Streaming songs are protected and no app can turn them into ringtones. Ringsmith works with your own videos, recordings and music files you own (for example in Files).

How do I remove a ringtone?

Go to Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone and swipe left on it. Deleting a sound in Ringsmith doesn’t remove a ringtone you already set.

What does Pro add?

Pro is a single one-time purchase (no subscription) shared with your family through Family Sharing. Everything you made keeps working whether or not you have Pro. To restore it on a new iPhone, open Ringsmith › Settings › Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Ringsmith upload anything?

No. Ringsmith has no account, no servers and no analytics; your videos, recordings and sounds stay on your iPhone.

▸ Ringsmith · Privacy Policy · Effective November 23, 2026

Your sounds stay on your iPhone.

Ringsmith: Ringtone Studio (“Ringsmith”, “we”) is built so that we never see your data. This policy explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Ringsmith does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Ringsmith makes no network requests of its own.

Videos, music and files

Ringsmith uses the system photo picker and file picker, so it only sees the items you choose. It copies the sound from them into Ringsmith on your iPhone so you can edit it again later. Those copies are removed when you delete the sound in Ringsmith or delete the app.

Microphone

If you record a sound, Ringsmith asks for microphone access. It records only while you are recording, and the recording stays on your iPhone.

Ringtones and alarms

When you set a ringtone, iOS stores it in your Sounds settings. If you set an alarm with your sound, Ringsmith asks for permission to schedule alarms; the alarm and its sound stay on your iPhone.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

Ringsmith does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
